#4471 is not one issue with one fix. The parser fix closes only the parser row. The other rows stay broken after it ships.

How the reports were told apart

Every count on this page comes from the reports at render time. Nothing here is typed in.

  1. One id, one report. The ticket export repeats some reports across its pages. uniqueById counts each report once.
  2. The tenant decides the cause, not the reporter’s browser. causes.ts asks each tenant’s locale and booking form. A report from a day-first tenant that came in through booking-v3’s form is hint. Any other report from a day-first tenant is parser.
  3. A one-day move is not a month flip. The desk’s notes (desk-notes.json) already mark the reports whose date moved by one day. The cause table files them under parser and footnotes them. Here they get their own row and their own ticket.

parser

374 reports from 11 tenants. Day and month swap: "Our 7 April reminder went out on 4 July." The shared parser reads every date month first, and every tenant here writes the day first. Only dates with a day of 12 or less can flip, which is why it looks random to the people reporting it.

Fix: parse dates in the tenant’s locale, not month first.

hint

33 reports from 3 tenants, all through the booking form, all at tenants on booking-v3. The symptom is the same swap, so it looks like parser. The cause is different: booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type what the hint tells them, month first, and the form reads it the tenant's way, day first.

Fix: make booking-v3’s hint and its reading agree (show DD/MM/YYYY for day-first tenants). A parser fix does not reach these reports.

one-day

5 reports from 5 tenants. The date moves by one day, and two people at the same meeting can get different days: "Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday." "The invite arrived before I made the booking." No day and month swap gives a one-day move. This points at time zones: a date-only value converted through UTC or the reader's zone.

Fix: keep booking dates as calendar dates, and convert only times, in the tenant’s zone.

Why one ticket hid them
