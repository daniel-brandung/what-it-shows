  • hint: won't fix, because the booking-v3 form is retired. Its ticket is closed as not planned.
#4471 is closed, but not everything in it is fixed. The engineering update does not mention the one-day row, and neither change moves a date back by one day. Its ticket stays open.

How the reports were counted

Every count on this page comes from the reports at render time, the way the desk’s own cause table counts them. Nothing here is typed in.

  1. One id, one report. The ticket export repeats some reports across its pages. uniqueById counts each report once.
  2. The tenant decides the cause, not the reporter’s browser. causes.ts asks each tenant’s locale and booking form. A report from a day-first tenant that came in through booking-v3’s form is hint. Any other report from a day-first tenant is parser.
  3. A one-day move gets its own row. The desk’s notes (desk-notes.json) mark the reports whose date moved by one day. The cause table keeps them counted where they are filed, under parser, and so does this page. They also count under one-day, so 5 reports appear in two rows.

parser

379 reports from 11 tenants. Day and month swap: "Our 7 April reminder went out on 4 July." The shared parser reads every date month first, and every tenant here writes the day first. Only dates with a day of 12 or less can flip, which is why it looks random to the people reporting it.

Fix: parse dates in the tenant’s locale, not month first. Shipped with #4471.

hint

33 reports from 3 tenants, all through the booking form, all at tenants on booking-v3. The symptom is the same swap, so it looks like parser. The cause is different: booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type what the hint tells them, month first, and the form reads it the tenant's way, day first.

Fix: none planned. The booking-v3 form is retired, so no new reports can come from it.

one-day

5 reports from 5 tenants. The date moves by one day, and two people at the same meeting can get different days: "Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday." "The invite arrived before I made the booking." No day and month swap gives a one-day move. This points at time zones: a date-only value converted through UTC or the reader's zone.

Fix: open. Keep booking dates as calendar dates, and convert only times, in the tenant’s zone.
